Terms of Use

Last Updated: July 23, 2026

Welcome to Pulse. Pulse is a school-focused communication platform designed to help students connect, communicate, and collaborate in a safe and respectful environment. By creating an account and using Pulse, you agree to follow these rules. ## 1. Account Requirements To use Pulse, you agree that: * You must provide your real first name when creating an account. * Your first name must accurately represent you. * Your last name is optional, but if provided, it must be accurate. * You must choose an appropriate username that does not contain: * Profanity * Offensive language * Hate speech * Sexual content * Threats * Impersonation of another person * You may not create accounts pretending to be another person. * You are responsible for keeping your account secure. Pulse reserves the right to remove or modify accounts that violate these requirements. --- ## 2. Respectful Communication Pulse is designed to be a safe place for students. Users must: * Treat other users with respect. * Avoid harassment, bullying, or targeted attacks. * Avoid spreading rumors or intentionally harmful information. * Avoid threatening, intimidating, or abusive behavior. * Respect other people's opinions and boundaries. Disagreements are allowed. Disrespectful behavior is not. --- ## 3. Appropriate Content Users may not send, share, or promote content that includes: * Profanity or excessive vulgar language. * Sexual content or inappropriate material. * Hate speech or discrimination. * Threats of violence. * Illegal activities. * Instructions for harmful activities. * Personal information about another person without permission. Pulse may use automated moderation tools to help keep conversations appropriate. --- ## 4. Chat Rules When using Global Chat, Direct Messages, or other communication features: * Do not spam messages. * Do not repeatedly send the same message. * Do not intentionally disrupt conversations. * Do not use chat to bully, threaten, or harass others. * Do not attempt to bypass moderation systems. Messages may be reviewed or removed if they violate these rules. --- ## 5. Username Rules Your username must: * Be appropriate for a school environment. * Not contain profanity or inappropriate references. * Not impersonate another user. * Not contain hateful, threatening, or harmful content. Examples of acceptable usernames: * `andrew123` * `luke_games` * `lucas2009` Examples of unacceptable usernames: * Usernames containing slurs * Explicit content * Threats * Fake identities --- ## 6. Friend Requests and Interactions Users should only send friend requests to people they know or have a valid reason to communicate with. Do not: * Spam friend requests. * Create multiple accounts to contact someone. * Use friendships to harass or bother other users. --- ## 7. Privacy and Safety Users should avoid sharing private information such as: * Home addresses * Passwords * Private account information * Sensitive personal details Never ask another user for information they are uncomfortable sharing. --- ## 8. Moderation Pulse may use automated and manual moderation systems to maintain a safe environment. Moderation may include: * Blocking inappropriate words. * Removing harmful messages. * Limiting spam. * Restricting accounts that repeatedly break rules. Moderation decisions may be made to protect the safety and experience of the community. --- ## 9. Consequences of Violations Depending on the severity of a violation, actions may include: * Warning * Temporary chat restrictions * Removal of messages * Temporary account suspension * Permanent account removal Severe violations may result in immediate account removal. --- ## 10. Agreement By using Pulse, you confirm that: * Your account information is accurate. * You will follow these rules. * You will help maintain a respectful community. * You understand that Pulse may take action to enforce these rules. Thank you for helping keep Pulse a safe and positive place for everyone.
